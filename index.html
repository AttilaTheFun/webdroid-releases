About this preview
This is a real Android-x86 4.4 guest, powered by v86. It supports older x86-compatible Android apps, not current ARM-only apps. Android renders in software inside the guest; WebGPU presents its framebuffer. Sessions currently reset when the page reloads. Internet inside Android and Google Play are not configured.
Boot can take several minutes, especially on mobile. Webdroid runs only while the tab is active.
